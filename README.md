# JL Translator - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

JL Translator (package app.translator.private) is a translation app by Justin Lim ("the developer"). This policy says what the app does with your camera, microphone and text.

## 1. What the developer receives

The app has no accounts and no server of its own. The developer does not receive your camera images, voice or text.

## 2. What the app does on your phone

- Camera: when you use the camera feature, the app reads text in the camera image using Google ML Kit text recognition. In the app's code this runs on your phone. The app does not save the images.
- Microphone: when you tap Speak, the app asks Android for on-device speech recognition only and does not fall back to online recognition. If a speech language pack is missing, the app can ask your phone's speech service to download it using the internet. Your phone may ask for approval. The phone's service handles the download, not the developer. Speech recognition remains on-device, with no online fallback. The speech recognition and text-to-speech services are provided by your phone's Android software. The developer has not tested every phone and cannot confirm what those services do on every device.
- Translation: text you type, speak or scan is translated with Google ML Kit Translation. In the app's code, the app does not write that text to storage.
- Summary test: The summary test uses a built-in English sample, not your meeting speech. Google ML Kit processes the sample on your phone. It may connect to Google for models and compatibility updates, and sends device, usage and performance data for diagnostics and analytics. This test does not save your audio or recognised speech.

## 3. Data collected by Google ML Kit (third-party software in the app)

The app uses Google ML Kit. Language models are downloaded to your phone over the internet. According to Google's documentation, ML Kit also sends diagnostic and usage information to Google over HTTPS: device and app information, performance data, installation identifiers, and the languages involved (for example the languages you choose to translate between or that are detected). This collection is done by the software included in the app, and the developer is responsible for telling you about it. The developer does not receive this data. Google's description and its handling of the data: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure and Google's privacy policy https://policies.google.com/privacy .

## 4. Permissions

Camera (to read text), Microphone (for speech input), Internet and network state (used by Google ML Kit to download language models and to send the diagnostic information described in section 3, and for speech-pack download requests to your phone's speech service).

## 5. Sharing and sale

The developer does not receive your personal data and does not share or sell it. Google ML Kit, which is included in the app, collects the information described in section 3 on its own behalf. The app does not include ads.

## 6. Retention and deletion

The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing for the developer to delete. Language models downloaded by the app are stored in the app's own storage on your phone, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings may remove them. Data kept by Android services on your phone (such as speech recognition and text-to-speech) and data held by Google are handled separately under those services' own policies; the developer cannot guarantee their deletion. See the Google links in section 3.
